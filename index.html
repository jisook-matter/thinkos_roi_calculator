How this model works

  • No double counting. Right-sizing removes whole collections first; route optimisation is then applied only to the collections that remain, so avoided lifts and reduced mileage never claim the same kilometre twice.
  • Diversion savings are the gate-fee differential between landfill (including levy) and recycling, applied to the tonnes moved by the diversion uplift — plus any rebate on those tonnes.
  • Time savings are hours released × blended admin cost. They are real cost savings only where the hours are redeployed or headcount is avoided; otherwise treat them as capacity released and say so.
  • Avoided penalties are a probability-weighted expected annual exposure, not a guaranteed saving. Zero this field for a conservative case.
  • Payback is implementation plus the first year's licence, divided by average monthly gross savings.
  • Sector benchmarks are indicative starting points for discussion, not a quote. Switching currency converts the figures at an indicative rate — every converted figure stays editable.
